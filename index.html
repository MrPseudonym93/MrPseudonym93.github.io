Pathfinder Opportunity Radar

Private · local-first · single-user

A personal job-discovery and market-intelligence dashboard.

Pathfinder helps its owner discover, review and retain information about relevant UK life-sciences jobs. The application runs locally on the owner's Mac and is not offered as a public service.

Job discovery

Collects vacancy information from configured employer career sites and selected Gmail job-alert messages.

Structured review

Sends vacancy-only review requests to the owner's Gmail account and imports structured review results from labelled digest emails.

Local storage

Stores job records, preferences and OAuth credentials locally. Pathfinder has no hosted user database.

Why Pathfinder requests Google access

Pathfinder requests read-only Gmail access to find configured LinkedIn alerts and Pathfinder digest emails. It separately requests send-only access to email structured AI-review requests to the owner's own account. It cannot delete, archive, label or edit email.
